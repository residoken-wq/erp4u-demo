## [1.0.0-demo] - 2026-08-15

### Added
- 🚀 Initial public open-source release of ERP4U.
- 🔒 AES-256-GCM Column-Level Encryption for PII data across 6 core entities.
- 🌱 Comprehensive Vietnamese mock business seed data suite.
- 🐳 Self-contained Docker Compose one-command demo stack.
- 🔔 Native Server-Sent Events (SSE) notification streaming.
- 📋 Modular ERP features: Sales, BOM, Production, Inventory, HR, Finance, Purchasing.
- 📄 AGPL-3.0 Open Source Licensing & community contribution guidelines.
